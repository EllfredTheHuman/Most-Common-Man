HOW TO PLAY

MOST COMMON MAN

Everyone gets the same prompt and secretly submits an answer.

Once everyone has answered, all the answers are revealed anonymously.

Everyone then ranks the answers from BEST to WORST.

ROUND 1–2 ×1
ROUND 3–4 ×1.5
ROUND 5 ×2

After five rounds, the player with the most points becomes...

THE MOST COMMON MAN.
