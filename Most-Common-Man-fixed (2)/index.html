HOW TO PLAY

MOST COMMON MAN

Everyone gets the same question and secretly enters an answer.

Once everyone has answered, the answers are revealed anonymously.

Everyone ranks the answers from most common to least common.

ROUND 1–2 ×1
ROUND 3–4 ×1.5
ROUND 5 ×2

After five rounds, whoever has the most points becomes...

THE MOST COMMON MAN.
